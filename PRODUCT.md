# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Yousuf Khan's experience, technical judgment, collaboration style, and suitability for senior software engineering roles. They often scan quickly before deciding whether to read deeper or initiate a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Present credible evidence of Yousuf's senior engineering ability and business impact. The portfolio should make his strengths easy to recognize quickly while providing enough detail for visitors who want to evaluate his work and professional references closely.

## Brand Personality

Credible, senior, pragmatic. The voice should feel technically capable, candid, composed, and grounded in real outcomes rather than self-promotion.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy gimmicks, generic corporate portfolio styling, exaggerated marketing language, dense walls of text, and interactions that hide important evidence or demand unnecessary effort.

## Design Principles

1. Make professional evidence easy to scan before asking visitors to read deeply.
2. Show seniority through clarity, judgment, and concrete outcomes rather than decorative claims.
3. Preserve the authentic voices of colleagues and collaborators.
4. Use progressive disclosure to control density without concealing substance.
5. Keep interactions restrained, predictable, and useful to time-conscious visitors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Interactive elements must work with a keyboard and communicate state to assistive technology. Reduced-motion preferences should be respected. Meaning must never depend on color alone, including highlighted phrases and focus states.

## Projects

- [Baby Phone — ABC & 123 Games for toddlers](https://apps.apple.com/sa/app/baby-phone-abc-123-games/id6823124937): iOS app, Sep 2026 to present. An educational iOS app for toddlers. He owns the product decisions, development, App Store delivery and ongoing maintenance. English and Arabic activities cover letters, numbers, animal sounds and pretend phone calls. Offline use, personalised family photos and voice greetings, parental controls and an in-app purchase. Reusable functionality sits in a shared Swift package, with unit and UI test targets. Stack: Swift, SwiftUI, StoreKit.
- [B1 Atlas — German learning and relocation planning](https://b1atlas.com/): Web platform, Aug 2026 to present. A platform that helps people prepare for German language exams and plan a move to Germany. He owns the product direction, development, deployment and maintenance. A multilingual site combines practical guides with interactive tools for exam selection, Ausbildung applications and Opportunity Card planning. Guided user journeys and a waitlist support a structured self-study course in development. Stack: Astro, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS.
- [SellEazyy — Shopify apps for merchant operations](https://selleazyy.com/): Shopify apps, Mar 2026 to present. StockPing and OrderPing, two Shopify apps that automate inventory monitoring and order notifications. He owns product decisions, development, deployment and maintenance. StockPing sends location-specific low-stock alerts, recovery notifications and daily summaries. OrderPing routes order notifications to the right staff through configurable rules. Work includes Shopify API and webhook integrations, background processing, multi-channel notifications and Shopify billing. Stack: TypeScript, React Router, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Prisma, Redis/BullMQ, Docker.
